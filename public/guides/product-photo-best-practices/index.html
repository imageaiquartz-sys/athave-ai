How to photograph products for better AI catalogue results

A good result starts before you press Generate. AI tools have more reliable visual information when the original picture is sharp, evenly lit and shows the entire product rather than only a small cropped detail.

Start with your product photo →See one-time top-ups

Who this is for

A practical checklist for anyone photographing products on a smartphone, including shop owners, boutique teams and online catalogue managers.

Why use Athave AI for this

Good originals reduce the need to regenerate, which can save paid image-generation calls. Most avoidable issues come from hidden details, glare and blurry source images.

How to get a better catalogue image

  1. Use soft, even lighting

    Daylight near a window or a diffused light source gives useful detail without hard black shadows.

  2. Preserve the complete silhouette

    Leave space around sleeves, soles, straps or accessories so the model sees all edges.

  3. Inspect the output carefully

    Compare labels, text, colour, seams and distinctive features with the real item before publishing.

Before you publish: quality checklist

  • Photograph a real back view for garments with zippers or logos
  • Keep a clean, high-resolution original for reference
  • Do not imply a generated angle is a verified real photograph

Common questions

Why do AI results sometimes change logos?

Small text and intricate shapes are challenging for generative systems, especially when the source is low resolution.

How can I reduce unnecessary image credits?

Select only the specific outputs needed and start with a clean source photo.

Should I photograph a second angle?

Yes, if the final result must accurately show an otherwise hidden section of the product.
