Front and back product photos for clothing sellers

Two pictures can answer very different questions. Front photos show the neckline, logo placement, pockets and shape. Back photos reveal rear panels, closures, seams and printed designs that may not be visible from the front.

Create garment front/back views →See one-time top-ups

Who this is for

For polo and T-shirt printers, uniform businesses, fashion boutiques and shop owners preparing apparel catalogue listings.

Why use Athave AI for this

An inferred back is not a substitute for photographing a garment’s actual rear. A second reference can make a catalogue image more faithful, while keeping the output request focused on only the views you need.

How to get a better catalogue image

  1. Take a front photograph

    Show collar, chest prints, placket, sleeve edges and complete garment length.

  2. Take a real back reference if available

    Reveal rear embroidery, seams, prints and colour blocking so the AI need not guess.

  3. Select individual view cards

    Generate Front, Back or both. Each card corresponds to one output and one image credit.

Before you publish: quality checklist

  • Verify any rear artwork that the AI inferred
  • Check that the sleeve trims match both sides
  • Keep your exact brand colours and sizing information outside of generated assumptions

Common questions

Does front and back require two credits?

Yes. Each separate selected output uses one image credit.

Can I generate a back without a rear photo?

The system can attempt an inferred rear view, but the resulting hidden details are not verified.

Can I select only a front view?

Yes. Select only Front to request one image output.
