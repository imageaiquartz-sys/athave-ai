Our services

In the Upload section, Athave AI accepts customer-submitted product photos for processing and delivery by our admin team. No AI generation occurs automatically when a customer submits a manual photo order. AI Studio is a separate generative-AI tool; its outputs must be checked for accuracy.

Pricing and allowances

The ₹499 plan lasts 30 days from a confirmed payment and includes 50 source-photo uploads. Each additional ₹99 add-on adds 50 uploads to that same active period, and can be purchased after the current allowance is used. Unused uploads and purchased add-ons expire at the end of the period. A new ₹499 payment starts a new 30-day allowance. This service does not currently enable automatic recurring billing.

Photo delivery

Uploaded photos remain marked Processing until our team attaches downloadable results. We aim to deliver results within 24 hours, but this is an estimate and not a guaranteed deadline. Each upload may have one or multiple results; the quality and accuracy depend on the source content and editing process.

Private workspace
