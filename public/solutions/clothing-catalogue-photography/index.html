Clothing catalogue photos from everyday garment pictures

Shirt and garment stores often need consistent front and back pictures across sizes, colours and designs. Athave AI can analyze apparel photographs and offer separate catalogue view cards for the garments it detects.

Create clothing catalogue images →See one-time top-ups

Who this is for

Designed for custom clothing shops, uniform suppliers, T-shirt printers, local fashion boutiques and businesses selling ready-made apparel online.

Why use Athave AI for this

If a model wears both a shirt and trousers, the studio can suggest both products rather than automatically creating every possible image. Select shirt front, shirt back or trousers views individually.

How to get a better catalogue image

  1. Upload front and optional rear references

    Use a well-lit photograph with clear sleeve, collar and hem details.

  2. Choose the clothing item and view

    Tap Front or Back for the specific garment you want to show.

  3. Review every print and seam

    AI-generated backs may be inferred. Confirm real branding, stitching and contrast colours before publishing.

Before you publish: quality checklist

  • Take a real back photograph when possible
  • Keep artwork and embroidered logos readable
  • Avoid cropping the garment cuffs and hem

Common questions

Can I make both front and back T-shirt images?

Yes. Select both view cards, which create two outputs and consume two image credits.

Can Athave AI keep my polo embroidery identical?

The system uses the uploaded references, but exact embroidery and tiny text are not guaranteed. Inspect them carefully.

Can I isolate a shirt from a person wearing trousers?

The analysis is designed to detect the separate apparel products and allow individual view selection.
