Make product pictures for WhatsApp and Instagram selling

Social sellers often take photos quickly at a counter, in a storeroom or during unpacking. Athave AI helps turn those originals into a more consistent presentation for sharing with customers.

Who this is for

For WhatsApp business sellers, Instagram storefronts, small retail shops and businesses creating shareable catalogues without a photo studio.

Why use Athave AI for this

Square WebP downloads are convenient for compact online catalogues and fast sharing; you can choose just one useful hero view per product.

How to get a better catalogue image

  1. Take a clean phone photo

    Use good light, avoid covering product edges and include distinctive details.

  2. Select the image view

    Let the tool recognize the product and choose the front or catalogue angle.

  3. Share with truthful product details

    Include availability, specifications and pricing separately; review the generated image first.

Before you publish: quality checklist

  • Avoid sharing images with altered brand details
  • Use consistent product names in your catalogue
  • Check platform compression after uploading
