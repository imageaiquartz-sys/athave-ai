AI product photo editor for your online catalogue

A product listing depends on clear, consistent photography. Athave AI is an online product image editor that uses AI to identify products inside your upload and prepare selected views for your shop or digital catalogue.

Try the AI product photo editor →See one-time top-ups

Who this is for

Useful for sellers who shoot on phones, small brands without an in-house photographer and teams handling large quantities of product listings.

Why use Athave AI for this

Unlike a one-click batch that generates every angle, Athave AI lets you select outputs before generation. That makes the number of image credits visible and easier to control.

How to get a better catalogue image

  1. Upload the best available original

    Choose a JPG, PNG or WebP photograph with the product clearly visible.

  2. Check detected items

    Confirm the automatically found items and tap the cards for the views you want.

  3. Download and inspect

    Review fit, patterns, hardware and logos; save each 1024 × 1024 WebP output.

Before you publish: quality checklist

  • Avoid blurred source images that hide labels
  • Use accurate reference photos for special fabrics or patterns
  • Review all AI-created details prior to sale

Common questions

Does Athave AI remove backgrounds?

The studio is designed to produce products on clean white backgrounds, using AI-based image processing.

Can it handle a model wearing more than one product?

It can analyze multi-product photographs, such as a person wearing a shirt and trousers, and offer separate selections.

Can I select only one view?

Yes. Select one view card to request one image output and one image credit.
