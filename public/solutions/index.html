Who this is for

Built for clothing retailers, Instagram and WhatsApp sellers, small e-commerce businesses, marketplace merchants and teams preparing digital catalogues.

Why use Athave AI for this

Start from the photo you already have. Keep control of front, back or angled outputs, use credits only for selected images and download compact square WebP files.

How to get a better catalogue image

  1. 1. Upload your photo

    Use a clear original of the garment, accessory or product; a second reference photo is optional.

  2. 2. Pick the products and views

    Athave AI detects possible products. Select only relevant catalogue views.

  3. 3. Review the result

    Download the selected images, then verify colors, logos and details before publishing.

Before you publish: quality checklist

  • Keep product branding visible and in focus
  • Photograph the product with minimal motion blur
  • Supply a real back view when accurate rear details matter

Common questions

Can one photo contain multiple items?

Yes. Detection can identify multiple objects, such as a shirt and trousers, and let you select specific outputs.

Does analysis itself spend image credits?

Analysis does not deduct image credits, although the underlying vision API has a small processing cost.

Are outputs suitable for marketplaces?

The output is square and designed for white-background catalogues. Always verify each marketplace’s current listing rules.
