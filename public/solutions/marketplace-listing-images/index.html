Product pictures for shopping carts and ecommerce listings

Product thumbnails shown beside a shopping cart, in an online product grid or on a checkout page should identify the exact item at a glance. Use an uncluttered angle, correct product colour and a consistent white-background image. Athave AI can help prepare those product photos for your shop; always verify that the image matches the item a customer will receive.

Who this is for

Useful for independent e-commerce websites, catalogue exports and sellers preparing listings for online marketplaces and shopping websites.

Why use Athave AI for this

Image credits are linked to the number of views you choose. A seller can prepare one main image rather than paying to generate several unnecessary angles.

How to get a better catalogue image

  1. Check each platform’s rules

    Some platforms specify white background, image dimensions or product-only composition.

  2. Select the primary listing view

    Generate the clean, centred view that best matches the source and platform needs.

  3. Validate before publishing

    Compare the output with the physical product and marketplace policies.

Before you publish: quality checklist

  • Never hide damage or misrepresent the item
  • Check the output against the platform’s latest product-image requirements
  • Upload additional real photographs when necessary

Common questions

No. Platform policies change and approval is determined by each marketplace.

Yes. You choose each output before running generation.

You can download your product images for your store, subject to your rights to the original materials and the service terms.
