Clean catalogue pictures for bags, caps and accessories

Accessories vary in materials, hardware and shape. A consistent product display helps buyers compare items even when the originals were shot at different times or with different phones.

Make accessory catalogue photos →See one-time top-ups

Who this is for

For fashion accessory retailers, bag stores, small electronics sellers, cap brands and entrepreneurs maintaining mixed-product digital catalogues.

Why use Athave AI for this

Upload a product picture, let the system identify it and choose the relevant front, back, side or angled presentation. Only the selected outputs are sent for generation.

How to get a better catalogue image

  1. Photograph closures and edges

    Show straps, handles, zipper pulls and other distinct features clearly.

  2. Choose the view needed

    Select the catalogue angle, front or rear based on the detected item.

  3. Confirm functionality and shape

    Inspect the position of straps and small details before sharing the final photo.

Before you publish: quality checklist

  • Show the whole product silhouette
  • Keep hardware and fasteners visible
  • Provide an additional image for complex side profiles

Common questions

The system can attempt common bags and accessories, including bag front and back views.

Can I create an earbuds catalogue image?

Yes. Small electronics and accessories can be presented as catalogue-style products.

No separate app is required to try Athave AI, though image results should always be reviewed.
