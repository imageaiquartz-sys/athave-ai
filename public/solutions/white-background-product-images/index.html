Create product images on a clean white background

White backgrounds keep the focus on the actual product and make a catalogue easier to scan. Athave AI prepares a centred product presentation on white, suitable for many store and catalogue layouts.

Make white-background product photos →See one-time top-ups

Who this is for

Ideal for store owners standardising product grids, online catalogues, inventory teams and sellers preparing different products in one consistent visual system.

Why use Athave AI for this

The output format targets 1024 × 1024 pixels and compressed WebP, balancing visual quality and file size for online use. The actual file size depends on texture and image complexity.

How to get a better catalogue image

  1. Start with a detailed source

    Keep all outer edges visible wherever practical and avoid harsh shadows.

  2. Select the needed view

    Choose front, rear or relevant product angles based on the detected object.

  3. Inspect the white backdrop

    Check for leftover shadows, cropped corners or small AI-imagined details before listing.

Before you publish: quality checklist

  • Confirm your marketplace accepts your image style
  • Examine white products against the backdrop so their edges remain clear
  • Keep brand labels accurate

Common questions

Will the background be pure white?

The generation request is designed for pure white. Check the exported pixels visually before publishing because AI generation is not always exact.

Can it produce transparent PNGs?

The primary download format is white-background WebP, not transparent PNG.

Will image size always be a fixed number of KB?

No. WebP compression produces different sizes depending on the product detail and image complexity.
