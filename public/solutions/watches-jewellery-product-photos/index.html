Professional-looking product photos for watches and jewellery

Small reflective products are difficult to photograph consistently. Athave AI can help prepare clean, catalogue-style presentations for watches and accessories when the source photo captures the important details.

Create watch catalogue images →See one-time top-ups

Who this is for

Relevant to watch boutiques, accessory shops, jewellery sellers and resellers sharing product collections on websites or social media.

Why use Athave AI for this

Choose a front-facing watch photo or a second angled view as separate outputs. This keeps costs under control and avoids extra images you did not request.

How to get a better catalogue image

  1. Capture dial and case in focus

    Reduce glare where possible and make markings legible in the reference.

  2. Select front or angled output

    Tap the needed view card rather than generating extra perspectives automatically.

  3. Verify precision details

    Check dial indices, inscriptions, gem settings and brand names closely before commercial use.

Before you publish: quality checklist

  • Use high-resolution details for small inscriptions
  • Avoid reflections that hide the watch face
  • Do not rely on AI to invent authentic stone settings or certifications

Common questions

Can it edit smartwatch and wristwatch images?

The studio can process watch-like products. Image quality depends on the photo and supported detection.

Does it preserve tiny watch numbers perfectly?

No. Tiny text and dial markers can change, so verify every generated result.

Can I choose only a front view?

Yes. A single selected output uses one image credit.
