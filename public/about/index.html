About Athave AI

The story and people behind Athave AI.

Athave AI is a professional AI-powered product image studio for online shops, small businesses and creators, transforming ordinary photos into polished catalogue images on clean white backgrounds, including separate front/back presentations for clothing, studio shots of shoes, earbuds and accessories, and individually downloadable 1024 × 1024 WebP files; details not visible in the original photos may be inferred and should always be verified before publishing. Athave AI was founded by Sabith Salah K P and is developed by Quartz Web Solutions, a Kerala-based digital company creating websites, e-commerce stores, business applications and custom digital solutions.

Built for real business product catalogues

Founded by Sabith Salah K P, Athave AI focuses on helping business owners create selected product-image views without unnecessary generations. The service supports catalogue workflows for apparel, footwear and accessories with white-background, downloadable WebP images. Quartz Web Solutions, the website and software development company behind the platform, works on websites, e-commerce systems and digital tools for businesses in Kerala and beyond.
