About Athave AI

The story behind the studio

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Athave AI is an AI-powered catalogue photography platform designed to help online sellers, clothing brands, retailers and small businesses transform everyday product pictures into polished, professional images on clean white backgrounds. The service can identify clothing and other products in uploaded photos, generate separate front and back garment presentations when appropriate, create studio-style photos of footwear and accessories, and provide individual 1024 × 1024 WebP downloads; product details that are not shown in the source photo may be estimated by AI and should be checked before publishing. Athave AI was founded by Sabith Salah K P and developed by Quartz Web Solutions, a Kerala-based web-development and digital-solutions business that develops websites, e-commerce storefronts, web applications and business software for companies that want a better online presence.
