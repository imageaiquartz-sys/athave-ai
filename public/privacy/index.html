Privacy Policy

What we collect

We process your mobile number for OTP sign-in, account name, optional shop/contact details, submitted product photos, manually delivered results, payment/order references and service usage. A secure signed browser cookie identifies your verified account. We do not store your full payment-card information.

Why it is used

We use this information to process and deliver photo orders, verify paid Pro subscription access, provide your download history, protect accounts from unauthorized access and reply to support requests.

Service providers

Cloudflare hosts the site and API; Supabase provides the database and private image storage; Razorpay handles payments. The optional AI Studio sends requested text and reference images to the configured Google Gemini API. Manual photo uploads are not automatically shared with Gemini for generation.

Storage, security and access

We use private storage and signed session checks to restrict download access. Admins handling your orders can view original photos and attach results. Account access requires OTP verification of your mobile number. Contact support with access or deletion requests and provide information sufficient to verify ownership. Payment/legal retention requirements may apply.
