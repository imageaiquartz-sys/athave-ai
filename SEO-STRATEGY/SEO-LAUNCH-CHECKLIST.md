# Athave AI — SEO Launch Checklist

## Day 1 — Deployment
- [ ] Confirm all new HTML pages give HTTP 200 and are mobile-readable.
- [ ] Confirm only indexable pages are included in `/sitemap.xml`.
- [ ] Inspect `robots.txt`, canonical URL, and Open Graph preview.
- [ ] Confirm private routes `profile`, `admin`, `results` have noindex meta or headers.
- [ ] Confirm logo URL and alt text resolve; test product images on slow mobile network.
- [ ] Verify page titles, headings and descriptions are not duplicated.

## Search Console
- [ ] Verify correct property for currently-live hostname.
- [ ] Submit `sitemap.xml` (not 1,000 keyword phrases or private routes).
- [ ] Request inspection of homepage, About, Solutions and 2-3 flagship guides.
- [ ] Watch Page indexing and Web Core Vitals reports weekly.
- [ ] After 2-4 weeks, refine pages receiving impressions for high-relevance queries.

## Brand building
- [ ] Link to Athave AI from the official Quartz Web Solutions website with a truthful product description.
- [ ] Keep product name and spelling consistent across real official channels.
- [ ] About page includes verified founder and developer identities.
- [ ] Publish original before/after images with consent and accurate captions.

## Search content quality
- [ ] Do not mass-publish one page per keyword. Build genuinely useful pages around intent.
- [ ] Add real customer questions and authentic product demo screenshots.
- [ ] Keep refund and privacy policies accurate and reviewed for operations.
- [ ] Measure search traffic and conversion, not keyword counts alone.
